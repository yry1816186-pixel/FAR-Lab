# Evidence Research

## Objective
Produce decision-changing evidence, not a generic report.

## Protocol

1. State the exact decision/question and what evidence could change it.
2. Establish freshness requirements and the authoritative-source hierarchy.
3. Search by capability/problem/failure mode/architecture primitive; expand terminology across ecosystems.
4. Use independent query families when the decision is consequential.
5. Prefer current primary sources: official docs/specs/repos/releases/source/papers/benchmarks. Treat README marketing and stars as weak evidence.
6. For claims that matter, record exact source, date/version/commit when relevant, and evidence ceiling.
7. Actively search contrary evidence, limitations, failure reports, stale assumptions and stronger alternatives.
8. Continue until **decision saturation**: new independent searches mostly produce known patterns/candidates or clearly weaker alternatives.
9. Converge to a decision, prototype need, or explicit unresolved question. Research that changes nothing is not sufficient progress.

## Output
For each material conclusion:

- claim / decision question;
- strongest evidence;
- conflicting evidence;
- evidence level;
- uncertainty;
- implication for FAR-Lab;
- next decision/action.

Do not fabricate browsing, source inspection, release state, license, benchmarks, or consensus.
