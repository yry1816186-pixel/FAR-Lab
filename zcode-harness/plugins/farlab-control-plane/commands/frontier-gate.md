Do not run this as a substitute for real work. First require the Acceptance Floor.

Then verify, from concrete evidence and the project's canonical frontier/control record, the mission-relevant dimensions:

- scientific frontier;
- engineering/reliability/security frontier;
- measured performance/cost frontier;
- researcher product/workflow frontier;
- architecture coherence/ownership frontier;
- evaluation/strong-baseline frontier;
- innovation evidence/ablation/failure-analysis frontier;
- ecosystem/OSS/protocol/tool frontier;
- provenance/reproducibility frontier.

Also require:

- an independent adversarial audit that did not materially author the audited surface;
- a problem-driven Frontier Opportunity Sweep at decision saturation;
- no unresolved materially valuable executable opportunity hidden as P2;
- a marginal-value conclusion explaining why remaining work is blocked, out of scope, deferred with reason, or low value relative to cost/risk.

Return only:

- `FRONTIER_READY` with decisive evidence and residual bounded risks; or
- `NOT_READY` with exact failed dimension/opportunity and required next action.

Never convert “good enough for competition” into frontier completion.
