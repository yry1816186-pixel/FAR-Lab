# Non-goals

| ID | Item | Rationale | Excluded users/scenarios | Re-entry risk | Reconsideration condition | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NG-001 | Determine scientific truth | Evidence integrity and plan conformance do not establish truth. | Anyone seeking a true/false oracle | Marketing pressure | Never as a generic claim; only narrowly validated metrics | Science+legal |
| NG-002 | Detect or adjudicate fraud/misconduct | Requires intent, due process, institution authority, and broader evidence. | Journals/HR seeking automated accusations | Severe harm and liability | Only decision-support under independent institutional process | Governance |
| NG-003 | Autonomous scientist | Current value is reviewable evidence, not autonomous discovery. | Hands-off research automation | Scope explosion | Separate product and validation program | Product |
| NG-004 | General-purpose coding/personal agent | Mature projects already cover this; it weakens focus. | Generic coding/chat/automation | Competitive feature copying | Never unless receipt workflow demands a bounded assistant capability | Architecture |
| NG-005 | Validate 28 scientific domains from fixtures | Domain labels and offline replay are not validity evidence. | Cross-domain certification | Demo inflation | Each domain gets profile, expert set, study, release | Science |
| NG-006 | Multi-tenant hosted SaaS in v0 | Current schema/auth/isolation and operations are inadequate. | Public internet/shared institutions | Revenue pressure | Proven tenancy, privacy, SRE, security gates | Platform |
| NG-007 | MCP/ACP/A2A/marketplace support in v0 | No first-loop requirement; protocols and supply chain expand attack surface. | Agent ecosystem integration | Trend pressure | Pilot-blocking use case plus conformance/security test | Architecture |
| NG-008 | Blockchain or token economics | External anchoring can use conventional signatures/transparency logs. | Tokenized reputation/incentives | Spectacle over utility | Only if a concrete threat cannot be met otherwise | Trust |
| NG-009 | Custom scientific DSL | Existing schemas and profiles are sufficient target mechanisms. | Arbitrary executable study languages | Lock-in/complexity | Demonstrated interoperability gap after pilots | Data architecture |
| NG-010 | Real-time collaborative editor | Receipt handoff can start file/CLI based. | Simultaneous manuscript editing | UX scope growth | Repeated user blocker after core loop works | Experience |
| NG-011 | Store raw sensitive datasets by default | Minimize custody and breach impact. | Central data-lake users | Convenience | Explicit institutional deployment with controller policy | Privacy |
| NG-012 | Replace peer review, IRB, statistics or legal review | FAR-Lab supplies bounded artifacts and checks. | Automated institutional decisions | Automation bias | Never; integrations may support human authorities | Governance |
| NG-013 | Hide uncertainty behind one score | Separate trust dimensions are essential. | Leaderboard/composite badge buyers | Simpler marketing | Never without evidence that comprehension and safety improve | UX+science |
| NG-014 | Mobile/messaging/always-on gateway | No evidence for first workflow and unacceptable identity/ordering surface. | Personal assistant channels | Competitor imitation | Validated reviewer workflow requiring it | Product |
| NG-015 | Compatibility with active V1 as “verified” | V1 lacks mandatory full bindings. | Silent legacy acceptance | User convenience | Read-only import with explicit degraded/untrusted status only | Trust |
| NG-016 | Generic/permanent “world-class,” “best,” or competitor superiority claim | Parity is version/task/platform/metric-specific and expires. | Marketing/checklist comparison | Competition pressure | Only doc 18 scoped wording after independent evidence | Benchmark council |
| NG-017 | Zero-knowledge proof platform in V2 | Selective disclosure first needs a clear threat/data model; ZK adds cryptographic/governance complexity. | Hidden-computation/general private claims | Privacy spectacle | Concrete disclosure use case cannot be met by withholding/commitment/access control and receives specialist review | Privacy/trust |
| NG-018 | Indefinite/forever verification promise | Algorithms, formats, roots, references, law and funding change. | Perpetual archive reliance | Trust marketing | Only a funded declared preservation horizon/profile with repeated recovery | Archive/governance |
| NG-019 | Batch verification in v0 | Current workflow has no approved manifest/order/checkpoint/aggregate-exit surface. | Bulk institutional automation | Convenience/scope growth | Pilot demand plus full IRG-024 contract and isolation/capacity evidence | Product/platform |
